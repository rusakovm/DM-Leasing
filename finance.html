EZ LEASE

At D&M Leasing we’re all about making things easy for our leasing clients. That’s why we came up with the EZ Lease: a

user-friendly lease that provides better rates, more flexibility, and exceptional service.

EZ LEASE FEATURES AND BENEFITS

  • SIMPLE PROCESS

    Simply fill out our online quote form and one of our representatives will contact you.

  • EZ PAYMENT

    Make no down payment with no payments for 90 days (for qualified leases)

  • LOWEST RATES

    The EZ lease offers the lowest rates by any company on a standard consumer lease.

  • NO TERMINATION PENALTIES

    If you decide to opt out of your lease, you never pay early termination penalties

  • MILEAGE FORGIVENESS

    No excess mileage penalty at scheduled termination with 5,000 miles or less over the contractual mileage allowance.

  • MILEAGE FORGIVENESS

  • EZ LEASE ADVANTAGES

    Buy, lease, or trade over the phone, free loan cars for emergency service, and more.

  • LOCALLY OWNED

    Our friendly representatives aim to provide you world class service.
